# MIKAYLA brand system

## Brand idea

MIKAYLA is the fashion intelligence layer for travel. It reads a city’s visual language, connects that language to the traveller’s itinerary and wardrobe, and turns it into confident, shoppable outfits.

## Emotional promise

Fit in while standing out.

## Voice

Use a confident, quiet, knowing voice. Write like a perceptive fashion editor who understands travel context and respects the reader’s intelligence.

Prefer:

- “Every city has its own dress code. MIKAYLA reads it.”
- “What people are actually wearing there.”
- “Your closet first. New purchases only where they add something.”
- “One city. Three price points. The same point of view.”

Avoid:

- hype, exclamation marks, and “amazing”
- “tips and tricks”
- “cute outfit”
- generic travel-blog language
- unexplained technical language

## Visual direction

- Editorial restraint inspired by high-fashion houses, not copied from any one brand.
- Black `#0B0B0A`, ivory `#F4F0E8`, paper `#FBFAF7`, warm grey `#77736C`, gold `#A9874A`.
- Display: elegant serif with high contrast and disciplined italics.
- Interface: clean grotesk or humanist sans serif.
- Use asymmetry and alternating image scale to create a magazine rhythm.
- Use square corners or extremely restrained radius.
- Keep motion short and purposeful: reveal, slide, crop, and focus.

## Image contract

- Prefer public creator or editorial references with a visible source link, user-owned uploads, or licensed partner feeds.
- Retain creator credit and original source access.
- Do not present Pinterest-hosted images as owned by MIKAYLA.
- Do not scrape or permanently store individual social images without permission.
- If live data is not configured, label the feed “curated public references” and show the last curation date.

## Page priorities

### Home

Explain the product in one sentence and move immediately into city discovery.

### Discover

Make the imagery the primary interface. Put city, source, recency, detected pieces, and shopping action close to each image.

### Visual Shop

Let a user upload an image, choose a city, see detected pieces, and compare luxury, contemporary, and accessible alternatives.

### Plan

Accept pasted plans, images, PDFs, and manual entries. Return a day-by-day, venue-aware wardrobe.

### Closet

Make upload and categorisation effortless. Show that clothes are reused before new purchases are suggested.

### Studio

Use distinct rows for tops, bottoms, shoes, bags, and accessories. Keep the complete look visible while each row changes.
